What is WrindhaOS? +

WrindhaOS is a personal productivity and growth workspace that helps you organise tasks, habits, studies, focus, and longer-term goals in one place.

Who is it for? +

It's built for passionate people — students, exam aspirants, working professionals, entrepreneurs, and lifelong learners.

Yes. WrindhaOS offers free essentials, with a Pro plan for people who want access to more planning and growth tools. Check the app for current plan details.
